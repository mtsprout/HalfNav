# HalfNav Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

HalfNav is a navigation app for Android and for iPhone and iPad. It helps you drive to a destination with spoken directions for just part of the trip, and it shows the route and your arrival time. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

**The short version:** HalfNav has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not receive or store any of your information. To find places, plan routes and draw maps, the app sends your location and searches directly from your phone to the map services listed below.

## What the app uses

**Your location.** The app uses your precise location to plan a route from where you are, to show your position on the map, to give turn-by-turn directions, and to tell when you are near your destination. During a trip the app keeps running in the foreground with a notification so directions continue with the screen off. Outside a trip or an arrival watch, the app uses your location only while it is open on screen, for example to centre the map and to favour nearby search results. It does not track you in the background otherwise.

**What you search for.** Place names and addresses you type are sent to the search service to find matches.

**Places you save on your phone.** Your recent destinations, your Home place and your settings are stored only on your phone. You can clear them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include this app data in your device backup in your Google account, under Google's own terms.

## Who receives it

HalfNav sends requests directly from your phone to these services. The developer does not operate any server and sees none of this data.

- **TomTom** (search, routing, traffic and road-work information). Receives the text you search for, your approximate or precise location, and the start and end points of a route. TomTom handles this on HalfNav's behalf to answer your requests. TomTom says it may also use aggregated or anonymized data to improve its search and routing services, and it does not publish a fixed period for how long it keeps request data. TomTom's privacy policy applies: https://www.tomtom.com/privacy/
- **OpenFreeMap** (map pictures). Receives requests for the map area on screen, and your device's IP address, as any website does. https://openfreemap.org/
- **Google Play services** (finding your location on Android). Used on your phone to determine your location. It is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Maps** (optional). If you choose to have Google Maps guide part of a trip, HalfNav passes the destination to the Google Maps app on your phone, and Google's terms then apply. HalfNav's own voice does not use Google Maps.
- **Apple** (iPhone and iPad). The iPhone and iPad app uses Apple's location and map frameworks on your device, under Apple's privacy policy.

Requests to these services are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## Android Auto

When your phone is connected to Android Auto, the same information that appears on the phone (your destination, route, directions and arrival time) is shown on the car's display and played through its speakers. The app does not send it anywhere else.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No advertising, and no sale of data.
- No analytics or crash-reporting services.
- No sharing of your information with anyone other than the services above, which receive it to answer your requests.

## Children

HalfNav is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission in your phone's settings, though the app cannot plan or follow a route without it. You can end a trip at any time, which stops the app using your location. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your phone.
